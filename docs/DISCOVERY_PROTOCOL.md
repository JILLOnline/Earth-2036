# Earth 2036 — Dynamic Universe & Discovery Protocol

## Objective

Earth 2036 maintains a **250-company active research universe** and an **unbounded external discovery pool**.

The active universe is a competition, not a protected list:

- Ranks **#1–10**: Championship Board
- Ranks **#11–250**: Contenders
- Outside #250: Discovery pool / challengers

No company receives permanent membership because of reputation, size, incumbent status or prior rank.

## Trial requirement

The Top 250 operates in trial mode until at least **1,000 full-universe research ticks** have been completed under comparable methodology.

A global trial count and a per-company observation count are both required:

- Global tick count measures methodology history.
- Company observation age prevents a late entrant from inheriting history it did not earn.
- New entrants can rank immediately when evidence is strong enough, but remain marked as low-history/rookie until they accumulate comparable observations.
- Completing the 1,000-tick trial validates the process; it does **not** freeze universe membership.

## Discovery principle

**The universe is capped. Discovery is not.**

The system continuously searches for companies outside the Top 250. A newly created, newly public, newly relevant or previously ignored company can challenge the active boundary.

## New-company detection

Primary discovery paths include:

1. SEC registration and filing activity: S-1, F-1, 8-A, Exchange Act registration, material filings and ticker/exchange metadata.
2. NYSE, Nasdaq and Cboe new-listing / IPO activity.
3. FINRA OTC Daily List additions and symbol changes.
4. OTC Markets development-stage and international-company coverage.
5. Federal procurement and awards through USAspending.gov and SAM.gov.
6. Patent-assignee activity through USPTO data.
7. Sector-specific primary sources such as DOE, NRC, FERC, NASA, FCC, FDA and ClinicalTrials.gov.

A discovery event creates or updates a record in the external discovery pool even when the company is not yet eligible for the active 250.

## Admission to the Top 250

An outside company is evaluated on the same Earth 2036 methodology as an active contender, with explicit data-confidence treatment.

Admission is based on evidence, not hype. The system compares the challenger against the current #250 boundary using:

- Earth Score
- data confidence
- evidence maturity
- risk
- strategic relevance / 2036 alignment
- bottleneck control
- cross-division leverage
- substitution exposure
- capital intensity
- demand confirmation
- scenario robustness

A challenger may replace the weakest active member when its confidence-adjusted case is stronger. Every replacement is written to immutable boundary history with the evidence bundle that justified it.

## No-history advantage

New companies are not penalized merely for being new, but they are not allowed to masquerade as seasoned evidence either.

The system separates:

- **Score** — what current evidence says.
- **Confidence** — how complete and reliable the evidence is.
- **Observation age** — how many comparable ticks exist.

This allows a genuinely exceptional new listing to surface quickly without pretending it has years of history.

## Source independence

Ten articles repeating one SEC filing are one underlying fact, not ten confirmations.

Evidence should be deduplicated by source fingerprint and traced back to the earliest reliable primary source whenever possible.

Preferred hierarchy:

1. Regulatory filings / audited reporting / exchange records
2. Government agencies and official procurement records
3. Standards bodies / scientific and technical primary sources
4. Company investor relations and direct operating disclosures
5. High-quality financial and industry reporting
6. Social/media/community sources for discovery only until independently confirmed

## Removal / archival

Companies can leave the active 250 because of:

- being outscored by an external challenger
- delisting or acquisition
- thesis failure
- deteriorating financial viability
- loss of strategic relevance
- data-quality failure

Removal never deletes history. The company remains archived and can re-enter later if circumstances change.

## Core rule

Earth 2036 is not trying to predict one future.

It is building enough history, enough source coverage and enough competing companies that the companies which repeatedly survive cross-comparison can earn conviction instead of being granted it.
