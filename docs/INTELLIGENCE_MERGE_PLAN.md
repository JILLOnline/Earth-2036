# Earth 2036 — Intelligence Merge Plan

## Objective

Absorb the strongest research, event-intelligence, alternative-data and market-data ideas without surrendering Earth 2036's methodology, memory, causal model or decision authority.

Permanent rule: **Earth 2036 keeps at least 51% sovereign decision influence. No single external provider may exceed 49%.** Public primary evidence and Earth-native transformations are sovereign inputs. Vendor analytics are optional sensors, never canonical truth.

## Preserve the current core

Earth Score 1.0 stays locked. Opportunity Score remains separate. The 250-company universe, unbounded discovery pool, append-only history, causal graph, T0 gate and 1,000-tick trial remain intact. This work strengthens evidence admission and observability; it does not silently change score weights.

## Patterns to absorb

- **AlphaSense-style autonomy:** persistent research context and continuous monitoring. Earth stores accepted findings in permanent evidence and causal history instead of disposable reports.
- **RavenPack-style event normalization:** entity/event tagging, novelty and temporal treatment. Earth collapses duplicate coverage to the originating event.
- **Dataminr-style early detection:** broad real-world event discovery. Detection alone cannot move a rank until it passes Earth gates.
- **Paradox-style sensor breadth:** search, web, social, commerce, workforce and eventually lawful physical-world signals. These are evidence candidates, not answers.
- **YipitData-style inspectability:** coverage, methodology, sample-size and freshness metadata for sampled data.
- **Quiver-style public-record breadth:** contracts, patents, insiders, ownership and other unusual public datasets, preferring the underlying primary record when practical.
- **Unusual Whales-style market behavior:** optional flow, volatility and positioning signals isolated from the long-horizon Earth Score unless a future methodology version explicitly changes that boundary.

## Intelligence architecture

### Sensor layer

Every observation carries provider/source id, authority class, control domain, source family, event/origin fingerprint, observation time, freshness SLA, magnitude, reliability, confidence and direction.

### Evidence fusion layer

Before influence is admitted, syndicated copies collapse to one origin, stale observations expire from active influence without deleting history, contradictions remain visible, extreme numeric jumps can be quarantined, provider concentration is measured, sovereign influence must stay at or above 51%, and no external provider may exceed 49%.

### Provenance and causal integrity

A publishable score must have factor-level source IDs and notes, at least one primary source, a real company node in the causal graph, sourced causal edges, and stronger origin/corroboration requirements at higher evidence tiers. Gold and Diamond require independent corroboration plus a sourced structural signal with an explicit falsifier.

### Source health

Health combines coverage, freshness, historical reliability, disagreement rate and current failure/degradation state. Required stale or failed sources fail closed.

### Calibration — next wave

After enough outcomes resolve, add forecast calibration by horizon, source contribution versus later truth, feature/source ablation, unique-information scoring, provider cost versus unique value and regime-specific reliability.

## Build vs ingest

**Build:** evidence ledger, causal graph, entity resolution, source health, contradiction handling, deduplication, scoring/ranking, forecast grading, source-value attribution, promotion/demotion logic and longitudinal memory.

**Ingest when efficient:** optional adapters for public-record aggregators and market-microstructure feeds. Every adapter sits behind Earth normalization and the 51/49 rule.

**Emulate before buying:** large proprietary news, transaction-panel, expert-network or physical-world feeds are never required for system correctness. Reproduce the useful concept with lawful public sources first; pay only after measured out-of-sample value justifies it.

## Red-team gate

The architecture is not clean until automated scenarios cover duplicate inflation, vendor dominance, external-only evidence, stale data, provider outage, contradictions, extreme spikes, corrections, late entrants, premium-feed loss, fake causal flags, source-less causal edges, factor evidence without provenance, and high evidence tiers without independent corroboration or falsifiable structural signals.

These tests run in CI. The hourly cycle ends with a live Beast integrity audit; a failed audit prevents the cycle from being treated as clean.

## Rollout

1. Evidence fusion, sovereignty policy, source-health scoring, provenance/causal audit, red-team CI and UI telemetry.
2. Normalized event store, cross-source origin clustering and contradiction queue.
3. Optional public-record adapters.
4. Optional market-behavior adapter isolated to Opportunity/expectation signals.
5. Forecast calibration and source/provider value attribution after enough resolved history exists.
6. Expensive proprietary feeds only after measured incremental value.

External sensors can make Earth 2036 smarter. They never become Earth 2036.
