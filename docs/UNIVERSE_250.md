# Earth 2036 — Adaptive Universe 250

## Purpose

The active universe is deliberately broad enough to test Championship candidates against strong alternatives while remaining small enough for deep, repeatable research.

The universe is **competitive, adaptive and non-protected**.

## Structure

- 250 active research companies
- 10 Championship positions after official baseline
- 240 contender positions after official baseline
- unbounded outside discovery pool
- 1,000 qualified full-universe ticks before methodology 1.0 is considered seasoned

The initial 250 are seeds. Seed order is not a rank.

## Admission principles

A company can enter consideration through any verified lawful public signal, including:

- new registration or listing activity;
- public-market uplisting;
- material government awards or procurement;
- patents and standards activity;
- regulatory milestones;
- unusual operating evidence;
- emerging control of a bottleneck, resource, technology or infrastructure layer;
- a new human need or behavior becoming economically material.

No market-cap minimum by itself protects or excludes a company. Tradability, evidence quality, liquidity, risk and data confidence are evaluated separately.

## Boundary competition

After official T0, the active boundary is #250 versus the strongest outside challenger.

The challenger must beat the incumbent on the locked confidence-adjusted comparison rule. Every admission/removal event preserves:

- incoming and outgoing identities;
- scores and confidence;
- boundary margin;
- methodology version;
- effective tick;
- reason;
- evidence bundle.

## Observation age

Every company has its own observation age.

A company entering at system Tick 800 starts with one observation, not 800. Observation age is visible as maturity context, but it does not create an arbitrary waiting period if the evidence is already strong enough to compete.

Maturity labels:

- `rookie` — fewer than 30 qualified observations
- `seasoned` — 30 through 999 qualified observations
- `trial_complete` — 1,000+ qualified observations

## No sector quotas

Divisions are used to understand function and coverage, not to reserve seats.

The system may contain more companies from one division if the data warrants it. A new division can be created when the existing taxonomy no longer represents an emerging technology, resource, infrastructure layer or human need cleanly.

## Discovery is larger than the universe

The Top 250 is a research-capacity boundary, not an information boundary.

Earth 2036 should continuously search outside it so a company can be discovered before broad market recognition. The system is expected to preserve rejected and removed candidates so later outcomes can be compared against past decisions.

## Baseline

Official T0 requires one comparable methodology-1.0 baseline across all 250 active companies. No official #1–#250 order is published until that run is complete and publishable.

The prior 10-company v0.1 snapshots are retained only as prototype history.
